About

I am Cyril Shih-Huan Hsu, a researcher working at the intersection of evolutionary computation, deep reinforcement learning, generative models, and intelligent systems design. My work focuses on how simple principles from nature can inspire algorithms for solving complex, real-world problems. My background spans academia and industry, including research in scalable optimization, applied AI development, and startup experience. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Amsterdam, exploring agentic AI and deep reinforcement learning for distributed and cooperative systems.
