About

I am Cyril Shih-Huan Hsu, a researcher working at the intersection of evolutionary computation, deep reinforcement learning, generative models, and intelligent systems design.

My research focuses on developing and applying state-of-the-art ML algorithms to address complex, real-world problems. I am particularly interested in how adaptation, cooperation, and emergence can guide the design of intelligent, data-driven systems.

My background spans both academia and industry, including research in scalable optimization, applied AI development, and startup experience. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Amsterdam, where I explore agentic AI and deep reinforcement learning for distributed, cooperative multi-agent systems in next-generation networking.
